An all-over field. Marks run off the edge. This file is the gesture, not the color-field stack.

Rules for an agent

  • All-over. No focal object, no figure, no face hidden in the lines. Marks must leave the frame, so the picture reads as a crop.
  • Palette: raw ground #c2b6a2, black #1c1a17, blue #243c66, red #8d1f2a, bone #d8d2c6. Enamel-flat. No metallic shine.
  • This starter is the gesture. Do not paint a stack of soft rectangles and call it the same file. That other half is Rothko's problem, not this one.
  • Density has to change across the field. An even scatter reads as a pattern, and a pattern is the failure.
  • Invent the rhythms. Do not reproduce a Pollock, a de Kooning, a Kline, or a Mitchell.
