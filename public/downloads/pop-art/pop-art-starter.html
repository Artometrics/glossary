A flat invented product, repeated. The dots are a pattern you draw, not a photograph of print.

Rules for an agent

  • Repeat one flat invented product at least three times. Outline it like a printed panel. No shading that pretends to be a photograph.
  • Palette: ground #f7f4ee, black #111111, red dots #e31c23, blue #0047ab, yellow #f5c400.
  • The dots are painted as a regular pattern (a Ben-Day rhyme). They are not a halftone sampled from a photo.
  • No celebrity face, no trademark, no soup can, no comic you did not invent, no flag.
  • Do not reproduce a Warhol, a Lichtenstein, a Hamilton collage, or a labeled commercial package.
