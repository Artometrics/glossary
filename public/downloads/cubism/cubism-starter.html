More than one view, then one pasted pattern. Shallow. Invent the object.

Rules for an agent

  • Show at least two views of one invented object (a rim from above, a side from the front) in a shallow space. No deep landscape.
  • Palette: ground #b7aa9a, planes #e6dcc8, #8d8478, #d7c4a2, line #5c534c. Almost no saturated color.
  • One rectangle is a pasted pattern, not a painted illusion of a texture. A stripe is enough. It must look like paper or printed cloth laid on.
  • Do not facet a face into a mask, and do not draw a guitar, a violin, or a newspaper masthead.
  • Invent the still life. Do not reproduce a Picasso, a Braque, or a Gris.
