A grown line, and an empty medallion. The figure is not borrowed.

Rules for an agent

  • The line should look grown: one continuous curve, varying in thickness, like a stem under tension.
  • The frame and the empty medallion are the study. Do not drop a borrowed figure into the medallion.
  • Palette: paper #f6f1e4, vine #1f4d3a, gold line #a0782c, a dull rose #c46b73. No neon, no flat primary grid.
  • Ornament has to connect. A curve that does not arrive somewhere is decoration for its own sake — cut it.
  • Invent the vine. Do not trace a Mucha poster, a Horta stair, a Guimard entrance, or a Mackintosh chair.
