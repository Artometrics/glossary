Horizontal, vertical, three primaries, white. No diagonal and no circle.

Rules for an agent

  • Only horizontal and vertical. If a diagonal appears, this study has become the argument that split the group, and it does not belong in this file.
  • Palette: white #f7f4ee, black #111111, red #e31c23, blue #0047ab, yellow #f5c400. No other hues. No gradients.
  • Asymmetric. Do not center a logo. The white is a color, not leftover paper. Vary it.
  • No circle, no type, no picture of a chair or a house. This is not the Bauhaus starter, which is allowed a circle.
  • Invent the intervals. Do not redraw a Mondrian, Broadway Boogie Woogie, a Rietveld chair, or the Schröder House.
