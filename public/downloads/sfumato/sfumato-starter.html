No contour. The edge is only a change of value.

Rules for an agent

  • Do not draw a stroke around the form. If a contour exists, the study has failed.
  • Build the edge from stacked transparent gradients, softer at the boundary than at the center.
  • Palette: warm ground #1a120c, flesh from #c4a484, #8d6b58, and #efe3c8, shadow #3a2a22, a gray-green distance #6e7c74.
  • The background meets the form. It is not a cutout.
  • Invent the head. Do not reproduce the Mona Lisa, the Virgin of the Rocks, or any other existing picture.
