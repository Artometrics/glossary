Separate touches. The color is not mixed in the shape. Invent the weather.

Rules for an agent

  • Build the scene from separate touches of color. Do not blend them into one gradient that hides the stroke.
  • Palette, kept apart: sky #d5e2ea, #f4d7a1, #c5d4df; field #7f9e7a, #c4b07a, #6e8f84; water #6f92a8, #355872, #d7e4ee; one warm note #c9843a.
  • No contour around a cloud, a bank, or a sun. The edge is where the touches stop.
  • This is broken color, not dots. Do not turn it into a pointillist field.
  • Invent the motif. Do not reproduce Impression, Sunrise, a haystack, Rouen, or any water-lily panel.
