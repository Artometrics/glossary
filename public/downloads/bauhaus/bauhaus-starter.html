study

Grid, primary color, lowercase. Not a redraw of a Bauhaus poster, and not Helvetica.

Rules for an agent

  • Geometry, primary colors, and black on an off-white ground. No ornament and no gradient.
  • Palette: ground #f3f1ea, red #e31c23, blue #0047ab, yellow #f5c400, black #111111.
  • Type is lowercase, a plain grotesque, and it sits on the grid. This is not a Bayer Universal revival and it is not Helvetica-as-costume. Say so in the file.
  • Every shape aligns to a visible or implied grid. If a form is only decorative, remove it.
  • Invent the arrangement. Do not redraw a specific Bauhaus poster, the Dessau building, or a Breuer chair.
