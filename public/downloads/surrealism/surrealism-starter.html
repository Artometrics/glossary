One familiar thing, described precisely, doing what it cannot. This file is the second method, not automatism.

Rules for an agent

  • This starter is the precise-impossible method. Edges stay sharp. Do not use automatic scribbles, frottage, or a dreamy blur.
  • One ordinary object, invented in the details, in a place it cannot be (here, a key with a hard shadow and no hand). Change the object if you like. Keep the contradiction to one.
  • Palette: sky #d7e0ea, ground #c8bba8, metal #c0c6ce, shadow rgba(60, 50, 40, 0.28). Academic clarity, not night.
  • The shadow must agree with one light. The impossibility is the object, not a mess of lights.
  • Do not reproduce a melting watch, a fur cup, a pipe with a caption, or any existing picture.
