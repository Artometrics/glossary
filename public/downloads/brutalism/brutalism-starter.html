Rules for an agent

  • Show the making. Board-marks, a joint, a thickness. A smooth gray rectangle is not this style.
  • Palette: concrete #8d8a84, a colder pour #b7b3ab, ink #1c1b19, a raw gap #d8d2c8. No wood, no marble, no glass mockup.
  • Type is heavy, structural, and a little too big. A mono stamp for the small labels (grid, level, pour).
  • The page is a section, not a texture sample. Include an edge so the thickness reads.
  • Invent the structure. Do not paste a photograph of the Unité, Boston City Hall, Hunstanton, or SESC Pompéia.
