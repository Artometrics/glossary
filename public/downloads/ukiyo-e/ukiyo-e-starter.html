Flat color, one keyline, the edge allowed to crop the shape. Not a copy of any print.

Rules for an agent

  • Flat color only. No gradient, no shading, no modeled face.
  • A black keyline of even weight. Color shapes sit slightly off the key, the way a block can misregister. Do this once, on purpose.
  • Palette, limited: paper #f4efe2, key #161616, Prussian-like blue #123a78, flat red #c83b2f, a mute green #2f6b4f.
  • Asymmetric crop. The subject may be cut by the edge.
  • Invent the scene. Do not redraw the Great Wave, Red Fuji, a Hiroshige station, or any other existing print.
