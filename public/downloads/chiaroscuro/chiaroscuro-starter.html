One beam. Everything outside it is allowed to vanish.

Rules for an agent

  • Most of the field is a true dark, not a dim gray.
  • One hard light, from a single direction. No fill light, no rim light.
  • Whatever falls outside the beam disappears. Do not draw it back in.
  • Palette: ground #070707, lit flesh #d7c4a8, lit cloth #8d1f2a, the beam itself a warm white that dies within a short radius.
  • Invent the figure and the room. Do not reproduce a Caravaggio, a Gentileschi, a de La Tour, or a Rembrandt.
